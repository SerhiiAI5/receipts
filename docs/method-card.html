how a swap log becomes a scorecard

What counts as profit, and what never does.

Every rule below is in the repo. Read it, disagree with it, change it.
What the log showsStateWhy
quote asset → tokenBUYcost basis added at average price
token → quote assetSELLrealized against that average, in time order
token → tokenSKIPPEDtwo unknowns in one trade — not guessed at
quote → quoteSKIPPEDmoving cash is not a position
position fully exitedCLOSEDscored. this is money that moved
position still heldOPENreported, never added to profit. that is a price, not a result
sell with no matching buyINCOMPLETEit arrived by transfer or airdrop — unscoreable, not free profit
log starts mid-positionINCOMPLETEleft-censored history cannot be fixed. marked, not guessed

The two that matter

biggest win% of gross profit in one coin
green weeks% of active weeks closed up
open / unscoreableshown, never counted
A big number produced by one coin is luck until it repeats. A number spread across positions and weeks is a process. That is the whole difference, and no leaderboard shows it.
